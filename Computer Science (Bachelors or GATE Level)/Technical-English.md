Phonetics is the branch of linguistics for a study of speech sounds and their production and qualities. International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) provides a standardized set of symbols to represent the sounds of a spoken language.

- Monophthong: Pure/Single vowel sound, made with a fixed tongue position and mouth shape throughout the duration of sound.
- Diphthong: Complex vowel sound that begins with one vowel sound and glides into another.
